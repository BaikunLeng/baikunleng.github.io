## D. Ranking policy

1. Hard gate: target move-in around Nov 20 (flexible Nov 15–25), and base rent + one parking spot <= $2,000.
2. A parking waitlist is **not** equivalent to available parking. Such a unit fails the parking gate until a spot is confirmed.
3. MFTE prices remain provisional until eligibility and approval timing are confirmed.
4. Distinguish property email quotes, public current listings, historical listing references, and estimates. Never merge these into one “confirmed” price.
5. Google rating and review count should be verified from Google itself; RentCafe, Apartments.com and ApartmentRatings must remain labeled as separate sources.
6. Noise/security/maintenance risks should be based on identified review evidence or actual unit orientation, not assumptions based solely on neighborhood or road proximity.
7. Do not declare a property the winner until unit, rent, parking, all recurring fees, move-in date and application/hold terms are sufficiently verified.
